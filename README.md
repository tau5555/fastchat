# FastChat

FastChat is a simple messaging app, similar in spirit to WhatsApp — built
around quick, secure conversations without the clutter.

## What it does

FastChat lets you create a personal account and log in securely, so your
conversations stay tied to you and no one else. Once you're in, you can
send messages to your contacts using their phone number.

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants a straightforward way to message people — no ads, no
unnecessary features, just a clean way to stay in touch.

## Why it matters

Messaging apps often ask for more than they need, or complicate a simple
task with clutter. FastChat focuses on the basics that actually matter:

- **Security first** — every account is protected by a strong password
  policy, so accounts aren't easy targets.
- **Verified numbers** — every contact number is checked for a valid
  format before it's used, cutting down on errors and mistyped contacts.
- **No distractions** — a lightweight experience that gets you talking to
  people without extra noise.

## Getting started

Create an account with a username, password, and phone number, then log
in to start chatting.
